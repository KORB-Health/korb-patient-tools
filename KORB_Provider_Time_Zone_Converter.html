What to tell the patient

Always say the time and the zone: “9:15 in the morning, your time in Phoenix.” Saying only the number is what causes the missed visits.

If you book an Arizona patient now for a visit after the change, the Central slot stays where it is in your schedule — it is the patient’s local time that lands an hour differently. Quote them the number this page gives for that date, not the number for today.

Why Arizona is different

Arizona clocks never move. Everyone else springs forward in March and falls back in November, so the distance between you and an Arizona patient changes twice a year even though nothing changed on their end.

The Navajo Nation in northeastern Arizona does change its clocks. For those patients choose Mountain instead of Arizona.
