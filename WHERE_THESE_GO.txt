THE ONE THING TO WATCH
korb-pharmacies.js exists twice, at the root and in Provider_Reference, and
the two must stay byte-identical. Both copies are in this folder and both are
already correct. If you ever upload one without the other, the tools and the
documents will disagree about which states Greenwich serves.

included so you do not have to run the build. If you later change the data
