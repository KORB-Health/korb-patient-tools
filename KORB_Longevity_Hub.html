Your Program Options

Foundation, Gateway and Peak

Treatment is peptide therapy, for goals like energy, sleep, recovery or body composition (your balance of muscle and fat). Peptides are small, protein-like molecules that act as signals in the body. You give them as small injections at home, in 16-week cycles, prescribed by your KORB provider. Most are not FDA-approved for these uses.

There are three program options, and you do not need to choose one. Your provider recommends the right fit once your baseline labs are back.

Words You'll See

Terms in your guides

Peptide: a small, protein-like molecule that acts as a signal in the body.

Growth hormone-releasing peptide: a peptide that prompts your own body to release more growth hormone, rather than replacing it. Sermorelin, CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin and Tesamorelin work this way.

Program option: one of three ways to structure treatment: Foundation, Gateway or Peak. Your provider recommends one once your baseline labs are back.

Cycle: a 16-week block of treatment, with a follow-up visit and new labs before the next one.

Add-on: an optional treatment your provider may add alongside your program, such as GHK-Cu.

IGF-1: a blood marker that reflects your growth hormone activity. It is checked when you take a growth hormone-releasing peptide.

Ceruloplasmin: the protein that carries copper in your blood.
