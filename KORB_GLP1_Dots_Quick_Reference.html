Who qualifies for the 1 mg low dose Metabolic Low-Dose Pathway

BMI is not the gate. Below weight loss criteria, the patient needs one documented diagnosis from this list, or qualifies for maintenance. BMI 20 or higher is the only BMI rule. Semaglutide dosing

  • Start 5 mg daily, 30-day starter or 90-day.
  • Increase to 10 mg after 4+ weeks if response is short and side effects are mild.
  • 20 mg only if 10 mg has stalled and is tolerated. Never a starting dose.
  • From injections: restart at 5 mg. Do not convert by equivalence.
  • 1 mg low dose is held. It does not step up.
  • No response at the highest tolerated Dot after a full 90-day supply: discuss injections or stopping.

Taking it and follow-up

  • One Dot under the tongue each morning on an empty stomach. Let it dissolve fully; do not chew or swallow.
  • Nothing to eat or drink for 15 minutes after. Missed dose: skip it, never take two.
  • Follow-up matches the supply: 30 or 90 days, never 60.
  • Each visit: weight, GI side effects, safety screen.
  • Verbal consent for compounded medication, documented in the note.
Say: "a low daily dose" · "compounded by the pharmacy, not FDA-approved" · "how much is absorbed under the tongue isn't known, so we adjust by how you respond"
Don't say: "microdose" or "microdosing" · longevity or living longer · "just as good as the shot" · any absorption percentage as fact
