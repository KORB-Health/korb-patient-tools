Base Program

GLP-1 Medication Therapy

For adults whose weight is affecting their health. GLP-1 medications copy a natural gut hormone that controls appetite and fullness. KORB prescribes compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide, made by a licensed U.S. pharmacy for your prescription, as a weekly injection or a daily oral form depending on your plan.

Why Patients Use It

Common Goals

Patients start for help with weight, appetite and portion size, and related goals such as energy and sleep. Results vary from person to person, and no result is guaranteed.

Getting Started

Your Follow-Up Schedule

Your provider reviews your plan every 4–8 weeks and adjusts your dose based on how you're responding. Your pharmacy is assigned automatically based on your state, and your label shows which one. The dose tool asks for it so it can show your exact syringe fill.

Testosterone Replacement Therapy

Base Program

Menopause Hormone Therapy

Bioidentical hormone therapy (hormones built the same as the ones your body makes), as a patch, cream or capsule, for hot flashes, sleep problems, mood changes, and other symptoms tied to hormone changes.

Welcome to Functional Health & Longevity

How the program works, from your first lab tests to the program option your provider recommends and starting your 16-week cycle.

Your Program Options

Foundation, Gateway and Peak

Treatment is peptide therapy, for goals like energy, sleep, recovery or body composition (your balance of muscle and fat). Peptides are small, protein-like molecules that act as signals in the body. You give them as small injections at home, in 16-week cycles, prescribed by your KORB provider. Most are not FDA-approved for these uses.

There are three program options, and you do not need to choose one. Your provider recommends the right fit once your baseline labs are back.
