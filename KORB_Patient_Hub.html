Weight Loss / GLP-1 Program

Medically-guided weight loss using GLP-1 medication, matched to your goals and monitored by your provider.

What It Is

GLP-1 Medication Therapy

Semaglutide or Tirzepatide, prescribed by your KORB provider and dispensed by a licensed U.S. pharmacy. Available as a weekly injection or a daily oral tablet, depending on your plan.

May Support

Steady, Sustainable Progress

Reduced appetite, gradual weight loss, and improvements in energy, sleep, and metabolic health markers over time — alongside nutrition and activity changes.

Getting Started

Labs & Follow-Up

Your provider reviews your plan every 4–8 weeks and adjusts your dose based on how you're responding. Your pharmacy is assigned automatically based on your state — no action needed from you.

Base Program

Testosterone Replacement Therapy

For men with lab-confirmed low testosterone and related symptoms — low energy, reduced strength, low mood, or low drive. Requires baseline and quarterly labs.

May Support

Energy, Strength & Mood

Improved energy, lean muscle mass, mood, and drive as hormone levels return to a healthy range — monitored closely by your provider.

Getting Started

Labs Required

Baseline labs confirm you're a candidate before starting, with quarterly rechecks to guide dose adjustments and keep treatment safe.

Women's Health Program

Hormone-guided care through every stage of perimenopause, menopause, and beyond.

Watch First

Welcome to Women's Health

What to expect at your first visit and how a hormone therapy plan gets built around you.

Base Program

Menopause Hormone Therapy

Bioidentical hormone therapy (patch or cream) to ease hot flashes, sleep problems, mood changes, and other symptoms tied to hormone changes.

May Support

Comfort & Quality of Life

Fewer hot flashes and night sweats, better sleep, more stable mood, and support for long-term bone and heart health when started in the right window.

Getting Started

Clinical Review First

Your provider reviews your health history to confirm hormone therapy is appropriate before prescribing, then checks in as your plan progresses.
