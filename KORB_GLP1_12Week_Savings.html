GLP-1 12-Week Savings

GLP-1 · Patient pricing · Provider quick reference

KORB Health Medical Texas PA · Clinical Operations · provider quick reference

What a patient saves by moving from a 4-week or 8-week supply to the 12-week program. Find the patient's dose and what they pay now; the last two columns are their savings.

How to read it

  • Per 4 weeks is about a month; programs bill in 4-week periods, 13 a year. Per year is 52 weeks.
  • The 12-week semaglutide price depends on the dose: compare a patient against their own dose band.
  • The 12-week program is filled by Premier only, and has no discounted rate.
  • Patients on a grandfathered 8-week rate are not shown here. Ask Operations for their comparison.

For KORB providers and Operations. Internal only, not for patient distribution. Every price on this page is read from korb-glp1-data.js and the charge-code file; nothing is typed here.
